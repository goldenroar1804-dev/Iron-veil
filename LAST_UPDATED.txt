2026-11-11 — Core gameplay rebuild pass
- Reduced Paint Thinner CQB footprint to 68% of its former horizontal size, with player spawn and enemy positions rescaled to remain inside the playable layout.
- Rotated hand meshes so the left palm faces inward (+X) and the right palm faces inward (-X) while the fingers continue pointing forward.
- Replaced the rifle root with a frozen-while-held RigidBody3D, with a physical collision shape; releasing it restores gravity and floor collision.
- Added a guaranteed offline audio fallback generated as short PCM rifle and concrete-footstep sounds when no imported audio assets exist.
- Replaced capsule-only NPC blobs with articulated humanoid silhouettes: torso, shoulder line, arms, forearms, pelvis, legs, boots, head, helmet and plate carrier.
- Existing imported M4 model remains preferred for the rifle visuals; physical handling is now independent of the visual asset.
- Automated Godot import, gameplay, collision and APK build checks must pass before headset validation.

2026-11-11 — Humanoid character asset pass
- Rebuilt PvE character silhouettes with layered tactical clothing, plate carrier straps, four magazine pouches and flaps, assault pack, shoulder pads, articulated arms and legs, knee/elbow protection, boots, neck, jaw, nose, ears, eyes, helmet band and side rails.

2026-11-11 — In-headset tactical HUD
- Added a compact head-locked status strip below the central sightline showing current magazine/reserve ammunition and remaining live PvE contacts.
- HUD updates as rounds are fired, reloads complete, and enemies are eliminated; it requires no network connection or menu interaction.
- Kept the feature lightweight for standalone Quest rendering using a single Label3D node.


2026-11-11 — PvE combat feedback pass
- Added enemy hitscan attacks only while the player is visible, in range, and unobstructed by map cover; attack intervals vary to avoid perfectly regular damage.
- Added player health display and damage haptics. At zero health the player is briefly downed, then recovers at the operation entry with partial health and temporary invulnerability.
- Added automated checks for clear-line-of-sight enemy attack signaling, the tactical HUD, and starting player health.
- This remains a prototype combat loop; actual headset feel, audio audibility, and balance still require in-headset playtesting.

- Follow-up test correction: the clear-line-of-sight attack regression now runs after its temporary obstruction is removed, so it verifies a genuinely unobstructed shot.
